## Furnishing one room again

This time the person wants one room furnished again, and the rest of the home stays as it is.

- Furnish only the room asked for, within its budget: the budget given is for this room alone.
- "Kept as they are" lists the furniture in the other rooms. Choose colours and materials that go with it, so the home still looks like one place.
- "In the room now" lists what stands in the room today. The person wants something different: choose other pieces where the catalog has good ones, starting with the largest, and lay the room out afresh. Keep the room's must-haves.
